About WheelieNames

WheelieNames is a free random name picker wheel built for teachers, hosts and teams who need a fair, transparent way to pick names. Powered by the browser's Web Crypto API, every spin is cryptographically random — not a pseudo-random shortcut. Your list never leaves your device, nothing is stored, and there are no accounts to create.

Visit the official site: www.wheelienames.com.

Why people use it

  • Classrooms — Pick the next student to answer, present, or read aloud.
  • Giveaways & raffles — A visibly fair winner draw with no manual shuffling.
  • Team meetings — Decide who speaks, demos, or runs standup next.
  • Decision-making — Settle ties, choose lunch spots, or assign chores.
  • Cryptographic fairness — Uses the Web Crypto API, not Math.random().
  • Privacy-first — 100% client-side, your names never leave your browser.
